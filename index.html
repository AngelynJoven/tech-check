Tech Check

Run this before your assessment call. It checks your internet speed, your work setup, and your computer. Takes about a minute.

STEP 1

Check your internet speed

Run the test at Speedtest.net (opens in a new tab). When it finishes, tap Share, copy the Result Link it gives you, and paste it below along with the Download, Upload, and Latency (Idle) numbers it shows.

Open Speedtest.net →

The Result Link is how we confirm the numbers are real, so don't skip copying it.

STEP 2

Your work setup

Tell us a bit about your setup. This part isn't detected automatically, just answer honestly.

STEP 3

Check your computer

Most of this is automatic. Processor speed isn't something browsers can read, so we just need you to check and type that one in.

Windows: Settings → System → About.

Mac: Apple menu → About This Mac.

Your browser can't detect this automatically, so just tell us.
